## Summary

This is a DESIGN phase (0 requirements; enables CAST-05 in Phase 6). It produces a **reviewable scene-template library** (Roadmap SC2: one MolAction sequence per stage type, each headless-prototyped with post-condition assertions) + a **reviewable cast-reveal convention** (Roadmap SC3: how a new enzyme first appears — full-vs-zoom, labeling, color language). The "cast representation at stages" question (CAST-05: "specific residue representations shown as the game proceeds via `cmd.show`/`cmd.hide`/`cmd.select`") becomes an explicit, reviewable spec rather than an ad-hoc-per-node invention inside the overloaded first-Qt phase.

The skeleton yields **6 scene TYPES** (within the 4-8 target), mapping all 55 nodes: **(1) preface** (intro/meta beats), **(2) substrate-traversal** (linear narrative progression, no enzyme focus), **(3) active-site-reveal** (the cast-reveal — enzyme first appearance, mutant→WT arc), **(4) branch-point** (destiny comparison), **(5) rng-shuffle** (citrate prochirality), **(6) ending** (4 tier-templates: true/good/normal/bad). Type (3) carries the cast-reveal convention (§C) and the sub-beats "substrate approach" / "product release" the roadmap names — they are temporal sub-steps WITHIN the active-site-reveal MolAction sequence, not separate types (the skeleton collapses approach→reaction→release into one enzyme node, e.g. `gly.pfk`).

**Top-3 recommendations:**

1. **Scene templates are MolAction SEQUENCES replayed on `on_enter`, NOT `cmd.scene` named scenes.** `cmd.scene` (`viewing.py:993-1044`) stores camera view + atom visibilities + colors + reps + frame as PyMOL *session state* that does NOT round-trip through the story-JSON save/load contract (Phase 6 SC3: "save mid-game and load restores the exact session" is satisfied by `on_enter` MolAction replay, the existing `c14/engine.py:163-194` pattern — NOT by `cmd.scene`). `cmd.scene` MAY be used in Phase 6 as a performance *cache* (recall a stored scene on re-entry instead of replaying), but the MolAction sequence is the source of truth and the contract. (§B.)

2. **Cast-reveal = full-structure-first (cartoon) → zoom to active site → show catalytic residues as sticks, with a colorblind-safe mutant→WT arc.** The enzyme "enters" as a full cartoon (the dramatic "meet the cast member" beat), then the camera pushes to the active site. The disease mutant residue is flagged **red SPHERES** (high-visibility "broken" signal) while WT catalytic residues are **green STICKS** — the representation redundancy (spheres vs sticks) makes the mutant/WT distinction colorblind-safe even though red/green hues alone are not. This composes with 5.3: 5.4 owns the BEFORE (mutant, red spheres, at the edit-allowed node `on_enter`) + the named color values; 5.3 owns the AFTER (WT-aligned restoration reveal at the branch node `on_enter`) which fills the `<mutant_color>`/`<wt_color>` slots 5.3 left open (`05.3-CONVENTION.md:318-319`). (§C.)

3. **A consistent named-color cast palette, documented now, consumed by Phase 6/7/9/10.** Enzyme cartoon = `gray`; WT catalytic residues = `green` sticks; mutant/disease residue = `red` spheres (colorblind-safe via rep redundancy, with `magenta` as the pure-hue-safe alternative surfaced as an open question); cofactor = `orange`; substrate/hero = `hero_color` (sibling-owned). Phase 10 owns the full palette, but 5.4 picks the cast defaults + documents them so the visual language is consistent across the ~20+ enzyme cast (Phase 9) rather than ad-hoc per node. (§C.)

**Primary recommendation:** Specify each scene template as a deterministic `on_enter` MolAction sequence (compose from `hide_all`/`load`/`show_as`/`show`/`color`/`select_focus`/`zoom` + one new `label` op + the 5.3 `align` op), headless-prototyped on `pdb:TBD_*` placeholder structures with `count_atoms("obj & rep <rep>") > 0` + `iterate`-based residue-count post-conditions. Keep it a **convention + template library, not a framework** — minimum contract surface only (mirror 5.3 §F).

## §A. The scene-type taxonomy (derived from the 55-node skeleton)

### Derivation method

Scene templates key off the **structural + gameplay role** of a node (Phase 5.1 SC5: `MC-choice` / `edit-allowed` / `both` / `neither`, `05.1-DESIGN.md:804-850`), NOT just its pathway stage. I read every node in `data/story_glucose/*.json` + the 55-row SC5 table (`05.1-DESIGN.md:915-971`) and grouped nodes by what the 3D scene must SHOW. The roadmap's 4 example types ("enzyme active-site reveal", "substrate approach", "product release", "branch-point comparison") map onto this taxonomy as: active-site-reveal (type 3, which *contains* the approach/release sub-beats), substrate-traversal (type 2), branch-point (type 4). Two roadmap-unmentioned types emerge from the skeleton: preface (the meta intro beats) and rng-shuffle (the unique `tca.shuffle` prochirality node); endings split into 4 tier-templates.

**Result: 6 scene TYPES.** Type (6) expands to 4 tier-templates at authoring time (9 authored templates total), but the *type* count is 6 — within the 4-8 target. Every one of the 55 skeleton nodes maps to exactly one type (multi-role nodes like `etc.entry` — both substrate-traversal AND branch-point — pick the MORE SPECIFIC type: branch-point).

#### Type 3 — active-site-reveal (THE CAST-REVEAL; §C owns the convention)

**Nodes:** 8 PDB-bearing disease-mutant edit-allowed (`gly.pfk`, `gly.pyruvate_kinase`, `pyr.pdh`, `tca.aconitase`, `tca.isocitrate_dh`, `etc.complex_iv`) + `tca.citrate_synthase` (structural variant, NO mutant) + 6 narrative-only edit-allowed (conditional, §A note)
**Purpose:** a new enzyme FIRST APPEARS — the cast-reveal. Full cartoon → zoom active site → catalytic residues sticks → mutant residue flagged red spheres (the disease the player will restore). Contains the "substrate approach" + "product release" sub-beats.
**Draft `on_enter` sequence** (disease-mutant cast-reveal; this is the BEFORE state of the mutant→WT arc, §C):

```jsonc
"on_enter": [
  {"op": "hide_all"},
  // 1. CAST-REVEAL: the enzyme enters as a FULL cartoon (the "meet the cast member" beat)
  {"op": "load", "target": "pdb:<ENZYME_PDB>", "args": {"source": "pdb", "code": "<ENZYME_PDB>", "object": "<enzyme>"}},
  {"op": "show_as", "target": "<enzyme>", "args": {"rep": "cartoon"}},
  {"op": "color", "target": "<enzyme>", "args": {"color": "gray"}},
  // 2. ZOOM to the active site (named selection the content author provides, Phase 7)
  {"op": "select_focus", "target": "<enzyme>", "args": {"name": "<enzyme>_activesite", "sele": "<enzyme> and <active_site_sele>"}},
  {"op": "zoom", "target": "<enzyme>_activesite"},
  // 3. SHOW catalytic residues as green sticks (the WT/healthy catalytic machinery)
  {"op": "show", "target": "<enzyme>", "args": {"rep": "sticks", "sele": "<enzyme> and <catalytic_residues_sele>"}},
  {"op": "color", "target": "<enzyme>", "args": {"color": "green", "sele": "<enzyme> and <catalytic_residues_sele>"}},
  // 4. FLAG the disease-mutant residue as red SPHERES (the "broken" signal; rep redundancy = colorblind-safe)
  {"op": "show", "target": "<enzyme>", "args": {"rep": "spheres", "sele": "<enzyme> and <mutant_residue_sele>"}},
  {"op": "color", "target": "<enzyme>", "args": {"color": "red", "sele": "<enzyme> and <mutant_residue_sele>"}},
  // 5. COFACTOR (if any) as orange sticks — distinct from green catalytic / red mutant
  {"op": "show", "target": "<enzyme>", "args": {"rep": "sticks", "sele": "<enzyme> and <cofactor_sele>"}},
  {"op": "color", "target": "<enzyme>", "args": {"color": "orange", "sele": "<enzyme> and <cofactor_sele>"}},
  // 6. LABEL: enzyme nameplate (headless-verifiable via cmd.label) + residue labels on catalytic residues
  {"op": "label", "target": "<enzyme>", "args": {"sele": "<enzyme> and <label_anchor_sele> and name CA", "expression": "'<EnzymeName>'"}},   // NEW op
  {"op": "label", "target": "<enzyme>", "args": {"sele": "<enzyme> and <catalytic_residues_sele> and name CA", "expression": "resn+'-'+resi"}},
  // 7. hero highlight step — sibling's op composes HERE (the substrate/hero in the active site)
  {"op": "zoom", "target": "<enzyme>_activesite"}
]
```

**Structural variant** (`tca.citrate_synthase` — `edit:structural`, NO disease mutant, `05.1-DESIGN.md:662, 676-682`): **omit step 4** (no mutant residue to flag). The enzyme is NOT diseased; the hero just explores it. The cast-reveal is otherwise identical (full cartoon → active site → catalytic sticks). This is the ONE active-site-reveal sub-variant: `structural` vs `disease-mutant`.

**Sub-beats "substrate approach" / "product release"** (roadmap examples): the skeleton collapses a reaction step into ONE enzyme node (e.g. `gly.pfk` = "PFK-1, the committed step"). The approach (substrate enters the active site) and release (product leaves) are TEMPORAL sub-beats that Phase 6 MAY animate by sequencing additional `show`/`hide` MolActions (or, for the MVP, fold into the single reveal). 5.4 specs the SINGLE-NODE reveal as the contract; the approach/release sub-beats are an OPTIONAL Phase 6 enrichment (a second `select_focus`+`zoom` on the substrate, then on the product) — NOT a separate scene type. Flagged as OQ-F6.

**Post-conditions (§D):** `count_atoms("<enzyme>") > 0`; `count_atoms("<enzyme> & rep cartoon") > 0` (full structure shown); `count_atoms("<enzyme>_activesite") == <expected_active_site_residue_count>` (the active-site selection has the expected residue count — Phase 7 fills the number, placeholder asserts `> 0`); `count_atoms("<enzyme> and <mutant_residue_sele> & rep spheres") > 0` (mutant flagged as spheres — disease variant only); `count_atoms("<enzyme> and <catalytic_residues_sele> & rep sticks") > 0`; label present (queryable — see §D).

**Side-by-side feasibility (5.4 vs Phase 6 split):** 5.4 specs the MolAction SHAPE — two named objects (`current` + `alt_destiny`) coexisting, different colors. **Post-conditions (§D):** `count_atoms("current") > 0`; `count_atoms("alt_destiny") > 0`; both `& rep sticks` visible. ## §B. The MolAction-sequence vs `cmd.scene` question

### The two options

| Option | What it is | Save/load-safe? | Consistent with existing pattern? |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|-----------------------------------|
| **MolAction sequences** (replayed on `on_enter`) | The story JSON's `on_enter` list of `MolAction`s, dispatched per-action to `molops.apply` (`c14/engine.py:163-194`, `molops.py:112-196`) | **YES** — the save/load contract replays `on_enter` MolActions (Phase 6 SC3: "save mid-game and load restores the exact session" via replay, the existing engine pattern) | **YES** — every skeleton node already uses this (`data/story_glucose/*.json` `on_enter`) |
| **`cmd.scene` named scenes** (`viewing.py:993-1044`) | PyMOL session state: stores camera view + object activity + atom visibilities + colors + reps + frame index under a key (F1-F12 / named) | **NO** — `cmd.scene` state is PyMOL *session-internal*; it does NOT round-trip through the story-JSON save/load. A saved game would lose scene state unless separately serialized | NO — would be a NEW mechanism parallel to `on_enter` |

### Recommendation (§B): MolAction sequences are the contract; `cmd.scene` is an OPTIONAL Phase 6 cache

**RECOMMEND: MolAction sequences (option 1) as the contract.** Rationale:

1. **Save/load requirement (Phase 6 SC3).** `cmd.scene` (`viewing.py:993-1044`) captures camera view + visibilities + colors + reps + frame as PyMOL session state — but NOT object LOAD state (it only records visibility/color/rep of *already-loaded* objects). So `cmd.scene` CANNOT replace the `load`/`show_as` sequence anyway (a recalled scene references objects that must still be loaded first). The save/load contract is satisfied by replaying the `on_enter` MolAction sequence (load → show_as → color → zoom), which is the existing `c14/engine.py:163-194` pattern. `cmd.scene` would be a SECOND, redundant state store that doesn't survive save/load.

2. **Determinism + reviewability.** MolAction sequences are pure data in the story JSON — reviewable line-by-line (the citation-gate per-claim review model, `05.1-DESIGN.md:319-326`). `cmd.scene` state is opaque PyMOL-internal; not reviewable.

3. **Consistency with 5.3.** 5.3 specified the WT-aligned reveal as a branch-node `on_enter` MolAction sequence (`05.3-CONVENTION.md:308-350`) — NOT a `cmd.scene`. 5.4's scene templates compose INTO that same `on_enter` mechanism. Using `cmd.scene` for 5.4 would split the reveal mechanism across two state stores.

4. **`cmd.scene` is NOT forbidden — it's an optional Phase 6 optimization.** Phase 6 MAY use `cmd.scene` as a PERFORMANCE CACHE: after the first `on_enter` replay loads + presents the scene, store it as `cmd.scene("<node_id>", "store")`; on RE-ENTRY to the same node (e.g. after an Observe→back navigation), recall `cmd.scene("<node_id>")` instead of replaying. This is a UI-tier optimization that does NOT change the contract (the MolAction sequence remains the source of truth; `cmd.scene` is a cache that can be invalidated/rebuilt from it). 5.4 specs the MolAction sequence; Phase 6 MAY add the cache. **Flag this as a Phase 6 discretion note in §E, NOT a 5.4 contract.**

### What 5.4 specs (the contract) vs what Phase 6 may add (the cache)

- **5.4 contracts:** each scene type = a canonical `on_enter` MolAction sequence (§A). This is what Phase 7 authors + Phase 6 replays. Deterministic, save/load-safe, reviewable.
- **Phase 6 MAY add (NOT contracted):** a `cmd.scene` cache layer for re-entry performance; multi-viewport side-by-side (5.3 §D, OQ-F3); Qt text overlays that SUPPLEMENT the `cmd.label` 3D labels (the nameplate in a corner — Phase 6 UI, NOT 5.4). 5.4 must NOT depend on these.

## §C. Cast-reveal convention (Roadmap SC3)

How does a new enzyme (cast member) FIRST appear? This section owns the visual design language for the ENZYME cast (NOT the hero — the sibling owns the hero highlight; AGENTS.md anti-confusion: the cast-reveal shows an ENZYME, the hero is a CARBON ATOM, keep them visually distinct).

### C.1 Full-structure-first vs active-site-first

| Approach | What it shows | Dramatic beat | Speed | Hero-meets-cast feel |
|----------|--------------|---------------|-------|----------------------|
| **Full-then-zoom** (RECOMMENDED) | whole enzyme cartoon → camera pushes to active site → catalytic sticks | STRONG — the cast member "enters" full-body, then the camera dives to where the action is | Slower (2 zoom transitions) | YES — "meet PFK-1, then look at its active site" |
| **Active-site-first** | skip the full structure; zoom straight to the active-site residues | Weak — no "meet the enzyme" beat | Fast | NO — the enzyme is just an active-site closeup |

**RECOMMEND: full-then-zoom (option 1) as the DEFAULT** for the 8 PDB-bearing edit-allowed enzymes. Rationale: this is an RPG following a carbon atom through a cast of enzymes — the dramatic "meet the cast member" beat is core to the experience (the spec's "cast" framing, REQUIREMENTS.md CAST-01). The full cartoon establishes the enzyme as a CHARACTER before the active-site closeup focuses the gameplay. The speed cost (one extra zoom) is acceptable for a narrative game (not a high-throughput tool).

**Tradeoff / when active-site-first wins:** for RE-ENTRY to an enzyme the player already met (e.g. `tca.shuffle` re-enters the aconitase context), skip the full-structure reveal (the player already knows aconitase) — zoom straight to the active site / substrate. **Phase 6 MAY implement this as a "first-visit vs re-entry" branch** (track visited enzyme_ids in GameState; first visit = full-then-zoom, re-entry = active-site-first). 5.4 specs full-then-zoom as the cast-reveal (first-appearance) contract; the re-entry optimization is Phase 6 discretion. Flagged OQ-F1.

**Exception — the 6 narrative-only edit-allowed nodes (§A note):** no PDB → no full structure → active-site-first is MOOT (there's no structure to reveal). They get substrate-traversal (current skeleton) UNLESS Phase 7 loads a structure.

### C.2 Labeling (headless-verifiable `cmd.label` + Phase 6 Qt overlay)

`cmd.label(selection, expression)` (`viewing.py:1332-1376`) labels atoms by evaluating a Python expression over per-atom properties (`name`, `resi`, `resn`, `chain`, etc.); clear with `expression=""`. **This is the headless-verifiable labeling option** — labels are atom properties queryable via `cmd.iterate` (the `label` symbol is in the label namespace, `viewing.py:1360`), so a post-condition can assert a label was set. Text overlays (the enzyme name in a screen corner) are Qt/Phase 6 — NOT `cmd.*`, NOT headless-verifiable, NOT 5.4's contract.

**RECOMMEND: two label layers:**

1. **Enzyme nameplate** (3D-space, headless-verifiable) — `cmd.label("<enzyme> and <label_anchor_sele> and name CA", "'<EnzymeName>'")`. A FIXED string (single-quoted in the expression, `viewing.py:1364` "All strings in the expression must be explicitly quoted") on ONE anchor CA atom (a stable, visible residue near the active site — Phase 7 picks the anchor). This is the cast-reveal's "name tag" — queryable: `cmd.iterate("<enzyme> and <label_anchor_sele>", "labels.append(label)")` asserts the nameplate text.
2. **Residue labels** (3D-space) — `cmd.label("<enzyme> and <catalytic_residues_sele> and name CA", "resn+'-'+resi")` — labels each catalytic residue with its name+number (e.g. "ASP-128"). This is the "specific residue representations shown" that CAST-05 requires — the player SEES which residues are catalytic.

**Phase 6 Qt overlay (NOT 5.4's contract, but 5.4 documents the intent):** the enzyme nameplate MAY be supplemented by a Qt text overlay in a screen corner (the "name + one-line role" RPG-style nameplate). 5.4 specs the `cmd.label` shape (headless-verifiable); Phase 6 adds the Qt overlay on top. The Qt overlay is NOT a replacement — the `cmd.label` is the floor (the game must work headlessly + the label survives save/load via MolAction replay; the Qt overlay is a polish layer).

**The `label` MolAction op (NEW — sibling-verifies, mirrors 5.3's `align` op spec):**

```jsonc
{"op": "label", "target": "<enzyme>",
 "args": {"sele": "<enzyme> and <label_anchor_sele> and name CA", "expression": "'<EnzymeName>'"}}
```

`molops` dispatches to `cmd.label(args["sele"], args["expression"])` — a direct `cmd.*` call like `hide`/`show`/`color` (`molops.py:114-131`), NO new injected dependency. **The empirical API verification + the molops dispatch landing is the sibling API researcher's aspect** (does `cmd.label` need `pymol.finish_launching()`? does the expression quoting work headlessly? — mirror 5.3's `align` verification). 5.4 RECOMMENDS the op shape; the sibling verifies + Phase 6 lands the dispatch (the 5.3 Q3 precedent: spec the shape in the design phase, land the dispatch in Phase 6, `05.3-CONVENTION.md:260`).

### C.3 Color palette (the cast color language — colorblind-safe via rep redundancy)

A CONSISTENT cast color language, documented now, consumed by Phase 6/7/9/10. **Phase 10 owns the full palette; 5.4 picks the cast DEFAULTS + documents them** so the visual language is consistent across the ~20+ enzyme cast (Phase 9) rather than ad-hoc per node.

| Element | Named PyMOL color | Representation | Rationale |
|---------|-------------------|----------------|-----------|
| **Enzyme (the cast member)** | `gray` | cartoon | Neutral, recedes — the enzyme is the STAGE, not the focus. Lets the active-site colors pop. |
| **WT catalytic residues** | `green` | sticks | "healthy / functional" — the catalytic machinery the player sees. |
| **Disease-mutant residue** | `red` | **spheres** | "broken / danger" — the disease the player will restore. **SPHERES (not sticks) = rep redundancy** so the mutant/WT distinction is colorblind-safe even though red/green hues alone are not (deuteranopia/protanopia cannot reliably distinguish red/green). The SHAPE difference (spheres vs sticks) carries the signal redundantly with hue. |
| **Cofactor** (NAD+/FAD/TPP/etc.) | `orange` | sticks | Distinct from green catalytic / red mutant — a third functional class. |
| **Substrate / hero** | `<hero_color>` | (sibling-owned) | The hero carbon + its substrate context. 5.4 does NOT pick this — the sibling hero-highlight researcher owns it. Scene templates leave a `hero_highlight` step where the sibling's op composes. |
| **Alternative destiny** (branch-point) | `<hero_color>` | sticks | The "where you could go" hint — reuses the hero color to signal "this could be you." |
| **Bad-ending broken state** | `red` | cartoon | The catastrophe/death signal (consistent with the mutant-red danger language). |

**Colorblind-safety note (the red/green pitfall):** red+green is the classic deuteranopia/protanopia failure. The palette mitigates this TWO ways: (1) **rep redundancy** — mutant = red SPHERES, WT = green STICKS (the shape carries the signal, not just hue); (2) **label redundancy** — the mutant residue is labeled, the catalytic residues are labeled (`resn+'-'+resi`). With both redundancies, `red`/`green` is acceptable. **Alternative (pure-hue-safe):** swap mutant to `magenta`/`purple` (magenta-green is colorblind-safe without rep redundancy) — surfaced as OQ-F2 (the human picks: strong-red-danger + rep redundancy, vs colorblind-safe-magenta). The 5.3 convention left `<mutant_color>`/`<wt_color>` as placeholders (`05.3-CONVENTION.md:318-319`); **5.4 FILLS them:** `wt_color = "green"`, `mutant_color = "red"` (with rep redundancy) OR `"magenta"` (pure-hue-safe) — OQ-F2.

**Named-color confidence:** `gray`, `green`, `red`, `orange`, `magenta` are standard PyMOL named colors (the palette exists in `constants_palette.py`; `molops_smoke.py:102` already uses `"green"` via `cmd.color`). HIGH confidence these dispatch correctly.

### C.4 The mutant→WT visual arc + composition with Phase 5.3

This is the KEY composition point: 5.4 owns the BEFORE (mutant, at the edit-allowed node `on_enter`) + the named color values; 5.3 owns the AFTER (WT-aligned restoration reveal, at the branch node `on_enter`). They compose via the `<mutant_color>`/`<wt_color>` slots 5.3 left open.

**The visual arc (the player's restoration journey):**

| **BEFORE** (mutant, broken) | edit-allowed node `on_enter` (e.g. `gly.pfk`) | Enzyme cartoon gray + catalytic residues green sticks + **mutant residue red SPHERES** (the disease). The cast-reveal shows the BROKEN enzyme. | **5.4 (this convention, §C)** — the active-site-reveal scene template (Type 3) |
| **[player edits]** | EditRouter routes (Phase 6 UI) | (no scene change — the player is in the edit UI) | Phase 6 |

The restored residue + the `<enzyme>_wt` overlay are colored **`wt_color = green`**. **Composition contract (5.4 ↔ 5.3):**

- **5.4 does NOT re-litigate the 5.3 align mechanism** (the `op="align"` shape, the `present` directive, the conditionality on mutant-reveal approach). 5.4 only defines the COLOR CONTRACT 5.3's reveal plugs into: which named colors mean "mutant/broken" vs "WT/restored", and that the cast-reveal (BEFORE) + the restoration reveal (AFTER) use the SAME palette so the before/after arc is visually coherent (red→green = broken→restored).
- **Hero-identity anti-confusion (AGENTS.md):** the cast-reveal shows the ENZYME's health (mutant vs WT), NOT the hero carbon's fate. At `pyr.pdh` the carbon IS shed as CO2 (correct decarboxylation) while the hero's electrons continue — the cast-reveal shows the PDH ENZYME restored, NOT the carbon becoming ATP. Keep the enzyme-health framing and the carbon-fate framing SEPARATE (mirror 5.3 §5, `05.3-CONVENTION.md:185-189`).

## §F. Open questions for the human design-review checkpoint

*(Mirror 5.3 §H. Each has: the question, the research RECOMMENDATION, the TRADEOFF/alternative, and a one-line "why it matters".)*

**Q-F1. Full-then-zoom vs active-site-first for the cast-reveal.** Should the cast-reveal ALWAYS show the full enzyme cartoon first (then zoom), or zoom straight to the active site?
- **Rec:** full-then-zoom as the DEFAULT (the dramatic "meet the cast member" beat); active-site-first as a Phase 6 re-entry optimization (track visited enzyme_ids; first visit = full-then-zoom, re-entry = active-site-first).
- **Alt:** active-site-first always (faster; loses the cast-member reveal).
- **Why:** the RPG "cast" framing (CAST-01) depends on the enzyme-as-character beat; speed is secondary for a narrative game.

**Q-F2. Mutant color: red (with rep redundancy) vs magenta (pure-hue colorblind-safe).** The mutant residue is flagged red SPHERES (rep redundancy makes it colorblind-safe) — or should it be magenta/purple sticks (colorblind-safe without relying on shape)?

**Q-F4. Ending-tier distinctness: 4 templates vs 1 parametric.** Are 4 distinct ending-tier templates (true/good/normal/bad) warranted, or a common skeleton + a `tier` color/rep parameter?

**Q-F5.
