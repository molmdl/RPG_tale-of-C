**Five headline findings:**

1. **1E79 is NOT "rat liver F1" — it is BOVINE F1-ATPase inhibited by DCCD** (X-ray 2.4 Å, Gibbons/Montgomery/Leslie/Walker 2000, Nat Struct Biol 7:1055–1061). The DCCD-inhibited state (ligand DCW covalent adduct) is a content-relevant caveat for the soul-jump climax node; the classic alternative **1BMF** (bovine F1, 2.85 Å, Abrahams 1994, Nature 370:621–628 — the Nobel-era structure) shows the three β-subunit nucleotide states (ANP/ADP/empty) that *visually demonstrate* the binding-change mechanism but lacks δ/ε. Verified alternative 7QSK is bovine Complex I + Q10 (NOT human, NOT ATP synthase — the task's "human complex I 7QSK?" guess is wrong).
2. **The J&F book already approved for TCA (LIBRETEXTS-METAB-TCA, CC BY-SA 4.0) has Ch 19 "Oxidative Phosphorylation"** with exactly the three subpages Phase 7 needs: 19.1 (ETC complexes I–IV + proton coupling), 19.2 (F0F1 ATP synthase + binding-change mechanism — **resolves the ATP-SOUL-07 "no dedicated LibreTexts home" flag from Phase 5**), 19.3 (regulation + **"the consequences of uncoupling"** — the OQ2/UCP1 + `bad.proton_leak` candidate). Chapter verified live today (subpage-level coverage confirm deferred to approval time, same pattern as the approved Ch 16).
3. **The chem.libretexts ETC-II page uses OUTDATED stoichiometry** ("three ATP" per NADH, "an ATP for every two hydrogen ions" — the deprecated 3/2 P/O values) and does NOT cover the F0F1 rotary mechanism. This is the strongest evidence for the **P/O-ratio decision**: recommend NO numeric ATP yields anywhere in game text; if yields are ever taught, the only in-hub source states the outdated numbers (documented conflict → contested-tier claim needing a strong external source).
4. **Two citation corrections vs `05.1-RESEARCH-disease-mutants-etc.md`** (live PubMed verification): Gaignard 2013 is **93(2):384–389** (not "93(4):704–709"); Pillai 2019 is **179(10):2138–2143** with title ending "...developmental regression, intellectual disability, and seizures" (not "1950–1956"). The claim substance (authors/journal/PMID/finding) is unchanged. Fix before any downstream doc copies 05.1's bibliographic lines.
5. **The CYC1 caveat resolves cleanly: BOTH W96C and L215F are real, UniProt-annotated MC3DN6 variants from the same paper (PMID 23910460), both ClinVar-Pathogenic, both mapped into 1BGY chain D.** The difference is only ClinVar evidence strength (W96C: 2013 review, "no assertion criteria provided"; L215F: 2025 review, "criteria provided, single submitter") — a human choice, not a correctness problem. **Primary recommendation:** Approve the J&F Ch 19 source batch (19.1/19.2/19.3) as the ETC + ATP-synthase + uncoupling source; keep the 5 verified bovine/pig PDB candidates for v1 (human upgrades 5XTD/9HZL/9I6F documented but not swapped — swapping invalidates the verified residue mappings and the whole ETC edit arc would need re-verification); avoid numeric P/O ratios in all text; reframe OQ2's "let go" choice as the real uncoupling/proton-leak branch (J&F 19.3) with the pure-narrative framing as fallback.

If the human wants a yield sentence in teaching text, it is a **contested-tier claim** requiring: a modern source verified at approval time (check J&F 19.2's subpage first — same approved book), plus an explicit "approximate, varies with conditions" hedge in text. Surface as Decision #6.

**Anti-confusion guard (binding, AGENTS.md + PROJECT.md row 107):** none of these claims may state or imply that the C14 carbon enters oxidative phosphorylation, becomes ATP, or becomes NADH. Electrons ≠ carbon. The ETC nodes' dramatic texts must keep the two framings separate: carbon → CO2 (shed at PDH/TCA, cross-ref); electrons → NADH/FADH2 → ETC → ATP (the soul). The skeleton's existing texts already do this — `end.true`'s "you are ATP" is the ONE borderline phrase (see §7, Decision #10).
