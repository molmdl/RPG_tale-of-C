Two skeleton-referenced candidate PDBs (7FS3, 6CFO) verified exactly as expected; 6CER (the V138M mutant) also verified.

**Top findings that change the Phase 7 plan:**

1. **NEW DISCOVERY — PKLR has a real disease-mutant PDB.** The Phase 5.1 research said "NO mutant PDB" for PKLR. Live RCSB verification today found **2VGG** ("HUMAN ERYTHROCYTE PYRUVATE KINASE: R479H MUTANT", 2.74 Å, Valentini 2002, PubMed 11960989; superseded ID 1LIY → 2VGG, confirmed via RCSB holdings API). `gly.pyruvate_kinase` can now use the same load-mutant-PDB approach (b) available to PDH (6CER) — a genuine design upgrade option for the human to decide.
2. **The LibreTexts "Pyruvate Dehydrogenase Complex" page is a STUB** (one Wikipedia figure, empty references — fetched live today). It CANNOT ground `pyr.pdh` claims. BUT section **16.01 "Production of Acetyl-CoA (Activated Acetate)"** of the **already-approved** LIBRETEXTS-METAB-TCA book (Jakubowski & Flatt Ch 16) explicitly covers "the oxidative decarboxylation of pyruvate by the pyruvate dehydrogenase complex (PDH)" — verified live via the Ch 16 hub's section descriptions. `pyr.pdh` claims can cite the approved source at a new section pointer, following the exact precedent of the approved TCA claims (which point at 16.02).
3. **ClinVar statuses verified and differ in strength:** PKLR R479H = **Pathogenic** (VCV000001510, multiple submitters, no conflicts, evaluated 2025-12-19); PDHA1 V167M = **Pathogenic/Likely pathogenic** (VCV000985548, evaluated 2023-12-21); PFKM G209D = **Likely pathogenic** (VCV003375341, evaluated 2024-09-20 — NOT Pathogenic). The PFKM claim text must be worded honestly: experimentally shown loss of activity (Raben 1995, verified via UniProt VAR_006066 evidence) + ClinVar Likely pathogenic.
4. **All three disease mutations are mechanically compatible with `cmd.alter`** (all missense; PKLR R479H is additionally annotated "no conformational change" by UniProt — the cleanest possible edit target). LDHA stays NOT edit-allowed (GSD11 canonical mutation is a 20-bp frameshift, Maekawa 1990 — mechanically incompatible; the only pathogenic point variant is nonsense Q100Ter — also incompatible; the missense alternative R171H is VUS). This re-confirms OQ-DM9 under the same mechanical-compatibility lens.
5. **The anaerobic branch is scientifically well-grounded on ONE page** — the LibreTexts Fermentation page (fetched live today, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) states verbatim: lactic fermentation "takes place in oxygen depleted muscle and some bacteria" (LDH, pyruvate→lactate, NAD+ regenerated); alcoholic fermentation is "in yeast" (pyruvate decarboxylase releases CO2, then ADH → ethanol, NAD+ regenerated); "If the supply of NAD+ is not replenished by the ETC or fermentation, glycolysis is unable to proceed"; fermentation energy yield "is much less than if the organism were to continue on through the TCA cycle and ETC". These four verbatim facts ground AN-G-02 (lactic/Good), AN-G-03 (ethanolic/Normal), AN-G-04/AN-ETC-02 (crisis/Bad) — AND the host-tension evidence for OQ1 (the source itself labels ethanolic as a yeast process).

Three content-layer options exist (§7.2); the recommended one (neutralize both conds + carry the O2 framing in labels/teaching text) needs zero topology and zero engine change.
7. **Known-edit "restoration branch nodes" do not exist yet.** The frozen 55-node skeleton has NO `*_restored` nodes — Phase 7 must either ADD them (topology change → Phase 5.2 extensibility convention + human re-approval + reachability-test count updates) or route known edits to existing nodes (zero topology, muddier narrative). ## 7. Anaerobic branch: OQ1 ethanolic framing + host_o2_low flag-setter design

### 7.1 OQ1 — ethanolic fermentation in a mammalian host (HIGH priority; explicit Phase 7 human decision, deferred from 05.1-06)

**The tension (now source-grounded verbatim):** the verified Fermentation page assigns lactic fermentation to "oxygen depleted muscle and some bacteria" and alcoholic fermentation to **yeast** ("The purpose of fermentation in yeast is the same as that in muscle and bacteria..."). A mammalian host does not run pyruvate decarboxylase. The skeleton's `anaer.ethanolic` node (one of the 21 frozen endings, `is_ending: "normal"`) carries the HOST-TENSION FLAG in its own teaching-text placeholder (line 71).

**Options with evidence:**

**(a) DROP `anaer.ethanolic`** — mammal host does only lactic + crisis (2-ending anaerobic branch). Normal tier remains reachable aerobically (`end.normal.co2`), so all 4 tiers stay reachable and the 1T+3G+2N+15B distribution becomes 1T+2G+1N+15B. **Cost:** topology change to the FROZEN, human-approved skeleton (55→54 nodes / 21→20 endings) — violates the Phase 5.1 freeze + Phase 5.2/5.1 test invariants (55-node + 21-ending assertions in `tests/test_glucose_reachability.py`), requires human re-approval, and discards a pedagogically valuable topic (ethanolic fermentation is the canonical biochem-lecture fermentation). The 05.1-06 human review explicitly APPROVED the skeleton including this node, so dropping it reverses a recorded human decision — bigger than a Phase 7 content call.

**(b) YEAST-COUNTERFACTUAL TEACHING MOMENT (recommended; zero topology change).** Keep the node + ending; frame honestly in both layers: the dramatic layer gives the player a vision of the alternate road ("in another host — a yeast cell — this is the road you would take: a carbon torn away as bubbling CO2, your body left as ethanol"); the teaching layer states the science verbatim-grounded: ethanolic fermentation is the YEAST pathway (PDC releases CO2; ADH reduces acetaldehyde to ethanol, regenerating NAD+); your mammalian host lacks this enzyme, so this ending is the counterfactual shown for teaching. Grounding: AN-G-03 (S-FERM verbatim, yeast-scoped) + AN-G-05 (the host-distribution fact — S-FERM verbatim). The CO2-released fact still maps correctly to the Normal tier (carbon body released as CO2 without the electron-harvest arc). **This converts the tension into the pedagogy** and respects: the frozen topology, the mammal/human host decision (the IN-GAME host stays mammal — the counterfactual is explicitly labeled as another organism), and spec.md's educator audience. **(c) HOST VARIATION** (mammal for lactic / yeast for ethanolic) — contradicts the resolved "Host = mammal/human" decision (PROJECT.md row 97 + the Key Decisions table). Listed for completeness; REJECTED unless the human explicitly reverses the host decision.

### 7.2 host_o2_low flag-setter design (blocks anaerobic runtime reachability)

**Current mechanics (verified in code):** `pyr.branch`'s two choices use `cond: "not flags.get('host_o2_low')"` (aerobic → pyr.pdh) and `cond: "flags.get('host_o2_low')"` (anaerobic → anaer.entry, with `effects: {"set": {"anaerobic": true}}`). The flag is set ONLY via `choice.effects.set` → `state.set_flag` (rpg/state.py:153-156; interpreter.py:143-151) — no choice in the graph currently sets `host_o2_low` (the anaerobic choice sets `anaerobic`, a different flag). Phase 6's own summary records: "the anaerobic branch becomes reachable when Phase 7 adds a mechanism to set host_o2_low."

**Options (content-layer only; the constraint prohibits engine/pymol_layer edits):**

**(A) COND-NEUTRALIZE + LABEL/TEXT FRAMING (recommended).** Remove (or neutralize) both conds on `pyr.branch`'s choices so both are always eligible; the PLAYER chooses the host's oxygen condition at the branch. Reframe the choice labels to make the science honest — e.g. "Proceed aerobically (the tissue is oxygen-rich) → acetyl-CoA → TCA" / "The tissue is oxygen-starved — ferment (anaerobic)". Teaching text explains O2 availability is a real, tissue-dependent physiological condition (hypoxic working muscle ferments — S-FERM verbatim) and that the player is experiencing/choosing the host's condition, exactly like the sprint-vs-jog physiology. **Pros:** zero topology change; zero engine change; a 2-cond edit is a CONTENT fix with direct precedent (06-05 edited these very conds as a content fix, no topology impact; the BFS ignores conds either way so reachability tests are untouched); maximum classroom playability (students can experimentally compare aerobic vs anaerobic in one playthrough — a teaching feature). **Cons:** the "air runs out" is player-initiated rather than host/RNG-initiated — the dramatic framing carries the load ("you feel the tissue's air thin — press on into fermentation?"). Scientifically defensible: the branch point (pyruvate fate ∝ O2) is real; WHO sets the condition is game framing.

**(B) EFFECTS-SETTER ON AN EARLIER EXISTING CHOICE.** Add `effects: {"set": {"host_o2_low": true}}` to one existing pre-branch choice (candidates: `gly.pyruvate`'s Observe choice, or `gly.pyruvate_kinase`'s Observe choice). **Pros:** keeps the cond-gate semantics (one playthrough, one condition). **Cons:** semantically incoherent ("Observe the pyruvate kinase active site" → causes hypoxia?!); steals the Observe choice's meaning; hidden state the player didn't agree to. Not recommended unless the human wants a forced-condition playthrough (e.g., wiring it to a NEW "sprint" choice — but a new choice on an existing node is a structural edit; a new NODE is a topology change).

**(C) NEW FLAG-SETTER NODE** (e.g. `gly.host_condition` between gly.pyruvate and pyr.branch). **Cons:** NEW node = topology mutation of the frozen skeleton → Phase 5.2 extensibility-convention territory + human re-approval + reachability-test count updates (55→56). Only worth it if the human wants a dedicated host-condition story beat (e.g., an RNG-weighted hypoxia event — the ARCHITECTURE.md line 481 concept). Note an RNG-driven host event would ALSO need an RNG-weights claim (high-stakes per-claim review) — significant added approval burden.

**Recommendation: (A)** — smallest diff, precedent-backed, and pedagogically strongest. Whatever is chosen, keep the cond syntax in dict-method form (`flags.get(...)`) if any cond remains (the 06-05 regex test `no-broken-dict-attribute-conds` scans for the attribute form).
