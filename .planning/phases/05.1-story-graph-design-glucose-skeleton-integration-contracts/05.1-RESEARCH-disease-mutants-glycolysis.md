- Alternative: `R39L -> L39R` (Ashkenazi founder — most common, but the residue is in the N-terminal region which may be less visible structurally)

**(5) Disease (narrative stakes):** Glycogen Storage Disease type VII (Tarui disease). Symptoms: exercise intolerance, muscle cramps, myoglobinuria after vigorous exercise, compensated hemolysis. "The muscle cannot commit glucose to glycolysis — the hero's host collapses on exertion." Inheritance: autosomal recessive.

**PDB caveat for human review:** The game's APPROVED cast PDB for PFK is 4PFK (bacterial, 2.40 A). To show a human disease mutation, three options:
1. **Keep 4PFK** + map human mutation to bacterial equivalent residue via alignment (approximate — the structure will have the right fold but the residue number label won't match literature).
2. **Switch cast to 4OMT** (human PFKM, 6.0 A) — correct organism + numbering, but too low-res to see residue detail.
3. **Keep 4PFK for structure + use AlphaFold AF-P08237 for residue mapping** — hybrid approach.

This is a science + narrative decision for the human (see 4 Open Questions).

**CRITICAL CORRECTION to current skeleton:** The current `pyr.pdh` cast candidate is **2OZL** (PLACEHOLDER_PHASE7). Recommended cast switch (for the replan): **Replace 2OZL -> 6CFO (WT, 2.70 A)** or **1NI4 (WT, 1.95 A, partial)** as the cast, and use **6CER (the V138M mutant, 2.69 A)** as the "reveal correct 3D model" reference / or apply V138M to the WT cast at runtime via `apply_edit`.

- **(b) Load-mutant-PDB approach** (unique to PDHA1): Skip the apply-edit; directly `fetch 6CER` to load the real disease-mutant structure. This gives accurate geometry (no `cmd.alter` approximation). The player's "restore" would then switch to `fetch 6CFO` (the WT).

Option (b) is scientifically stronger because the geometry is real (not approximate from `cmd.alter`). Per spec.md item 4 ("include option to restore or reveal correct 3D model"), the mutant PDB 6CER + WT PDB 6CFO pair is the cleanest setup. This is a design decision for the replan (and Phase 7 content).

**(4) Reverse-mutation (the player's "correct fix"):** `M138V` (mature / PDB numbering) = `M167V` (UniProt precursor numbering). The `edits.json` signature:

**(3) Can the mutation be applied at runtime via `cmd.alter`?** This is the crux of the problem:
- For a **MISSENSE** mutation (R171H, K222E, R315C, G60R, N88D): YES mechanically — `cmd.alter("resi 171", "resn='HIS'")` works. But the only missense variants are either VUS (R171H, G60R, N88D) or NOT disease-labelled (K222E, R315C). None are confirmed pathogenic.
- For the **NONSENSE** Q100Ter (the one confirmed Pathogenic point mutation): **NO — mechanically incompatible with `cmd.alter`.** The disease state is a TRUNCATED protein (residues 101-332 missing). `cmd.alter` only changes existing residues; it cannot represent a truncation. The `on_enter` pre-edit would need to `cmd.remove("resi 101-332")` (delete residues), and the player's "reverse mutation" cannot un-delete 232 residues. ## 4. Open Questions for Human Review

### 4.1 PFK cast PDB: bacterial (4PFK) vs human (4OMT) vs hybrid
The APPROVED cast for PFK is 4PFK (bacterial, 2.40 A). Human PFKM disease mutations (e.g., G209D) are numbered by the human sequence. Three options:
1. **Keep 4PFK + map the human mutation to the bacterial equivalent residue** via sequence alignment (the residue number shown to the player won't match the literature, but the fold and the structural effect are conserved — PFK is highly conserved across species).
2. **Switch cast to 4OMT** (human PFKM, 6.0 A) — correct organism + numbering, but too low-res for residue detail.
3. **Keep 4PFK for structure + use AlphaFold AF-P08237 model for residue-number reference** — hybrid.

**Recommendation:** Option 1 is simplest and the structural conservation of PFK makes it defensible — but the `text_teaching` must clarify "we are showing a bacterial PFK structure as a model for the human enzyme; the mutation shown is at the equivalent position." Option 2 has correct numbering but the 6 A structure won't show side-chain detail. **Human decision needed.**

### 4.2 PKLR R479W (REPLAN-CONTEXT) vs R479H (UniProt verified)
The REPLAN-CONTEXT section 2 example states "PKLR R479W" but UniProt P30613 + the Valentini 2002 structural paper + the Kanno 1994 Amish paper all show the Amish mutation is **R479H** (dbSNP:rs118204085). R479W does not appear in UniProt's curated disease-variant list. **The verified mutation is R479H; the reverse-mutation is H479R.** If the human specifically wants R479W, it would need separate verification (it may exist in ClinVar but isn't in UniProt's reviewed Swiss-Prot entry — lower-confidence).

### 4.3 PDH cast PDB: 2OZL (current, phospho-mimic) vs 6CFO (WT) vs 1NI4 (WT)
The current `pyr.pdh` cast candidate is 2OZL — confirmed via RCSB to be the **S264E phospho-mimic mutant** (engineered to mimic regulatory phosphorylation, NOT a wild-type or disease structure). This is inappropriate for the restoration narrative. Recommended switch:
- **6CFO** (2.70 A, WT + substrate analog) — proper WT, same paper as the mutant 6CER
- **1NI4** (1.95 A, WT, partial — residues 30-309 only)
- **6CER** (2.69 A, the V138M mutant) — usable as the "reveal correct 3D model" reference

**Recommendation:** Use 6CFO as the WT cast + 6CER as the mutant reference (option (b) from section 2.3 (3)) — gives real geometry for both states, no `cmd.alter` approximation needed. **Human decision needed.**

### 4.4 LDHA — make `edit:allowed` with a VUS, or leave as a normal story node?
GSD11 is a real disease but there is no confirmed pathogenic **missense** point mutation that fits the `cmd.alter` edit mechanic. Options:
1. **Leave `anaer.ldh` as a normal story node (no edit)** — recommended. The player still passes through LDH (lactate fermentation is the anaerobic ending branch); the node just doesn't have the restore-the-enzyme mechanic.
2. **Make `edit:allowed` using Q100Ter (nonsense)** — would require extending the Phase 4 EditRouter to handle truncation edits (delete + restore residues), out of scope for the Phase 5.1 replan.
3. **Make `edit:allowed` using R171H (VUS)** — scientifically weak; violates the no-fabricated-science rule by treating a VUS as confirmed pathogenic. Not recommended.
4. **Make `edit:allowed` using a non-GSD11 LDHA mutation** (e.g., a cancer-associated somatic mutation) — but the narrative frame is "save the sick host," and cancer somatic mutations don't fit the host-organism-sick framing.

**Recommendation:** Option 1. **Human decision needed** — this is a science + narrative + mechanic decision.

### 4.5 PFK mutation choice (which of the 9 to feature)
If `gly.pfk` keeps its edit-allowed status with a verified disease mutation, which mutation should be the "featured" restoration target?
- **G209D** — cleanest mechanism (loss of activity shown experimentally, dbSNP:rs767265360, PubMed:7825568); reverse D209G
- **W686C** — Japanese mild-form (narrative variance; reverse C686W)
- **R39L** — Ashkenazi founder (most common, but residue 39 is in the less-structurally-visible N-terminal region)

**Recommendation:** G209D (cleanest mechanism + experimental evidence of activity loss). **Human decision.**

### 4.6 PKLR cast PDB choice: 7FS3 (1.66 A, L-type) vs 2VGB (2.73 A, R-type disease-paper)
7FS3 is the current candidate (finest resolution) but is the L-type (liver) isoform; 2VGB is from the Valentini 2002 paper that specifically characterized the R-type (RBC) disease variants (including R479H). The disease (CNSHA2) is an RBC disease (R-type deficiency). Using 2VGB would tie the cast to the disease-variant literature directly; 7FS3 has better resolution. Both use UniProt P30613 numbering so residue 479 maps the same way. **Human decision.**

### 4.7 Numbering convention for `edits.json` and player-facing text
UniProt precursor numbering (with transit peptide) vs mature protein numbering (PDB chain) differ for PDHA1 (V167M UniProt = V138M PDB). The `edits.json` signature MUST use the cast PDB's residue numbering (what `cmd.alter` will see). The `text_teaching` should clarify which numbering is shown to the player (PDB residue vs literature/UniProt) to avoid confusion. **Flag for Phase 7 content.**
