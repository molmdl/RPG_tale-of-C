## 1. Citrate synthase (CS) — `tca.citrate_synthase`

**Gene:** CS (UniProt O75390, Citrate synthase mitochondrial, EC 2.3.3.1)
**Current skeleton PDB:** 1CSC (Gallus gallus, 1.70Å, APPROVED CAST-CSC-PDB-01)
**Already edit-allowed:** YES (pre-restoration-narrative; tag is for structural exploration)

### Findings

1. **Known disease POINT mutation?** **NO.** UniProt O75390 disease field is **empty** (no `Involvement in disease` block). ClinVar has 53 CS records; the 7 Pathogenic/Likely-pathogenic records are ALL **structural variants**:
   - `NC_000012.12:g.56173943_56407078inv` — a **24 Mb inversion** (OBJ_TYPE: Inversion), Pathogenic, Coffin-Siris syndrome 8 (OMIM 619328). NOT a point mutation.
   - 5 records are **copy-number gains** (whole-chromosome-12 trisomy / partial gains), Pathogenic, "See cases". NOT point mutations.
   - `4857746` — "Single allele", Likely pathogenic, **Inversion** (chr12:41506669-65826047), Silver-Russell syndrome 5 (OMIM 618908). NOT a point mutation.
   - The missense records (e.g. `p.Lys382Glu`, `p.Lys14Glu`, `p.Met67Ile`, `p.Tyr80Cys`, `p.Asn400Ser`) are all **Uncertain significance** or unclassified.
   - Conclusion: **CS has NO confirmed disease-causing point mutation** (missense/nonsense in the coding sequence). The disease associations (SRS5, CSS8) are caused by large structural rearrangements, which are NOT implementable via the `cmd.alter` point-mutation edit mechanic.

2. **Mutant PDB?** n/a (no qualifying point mutant).
3. **Reverse-mutation?** n/a.
4. **Disease for narrative?** n/a for a point mutation. (The structural-inversion Silver-Russell association exists but cannot be represented by a single-residue edit.)
5. **Source/claim_id CANDIDATE:** No claim_id to file (negative finding). Sources: ClinVar (gene CS, all 7 pathogenic records are structural variants); UniProt O75390 (no disease block); OMIM 618908 (SRS5), 619328 (CSS8).

### Recommendation
**Do NOT add a disease-mutant `edits.json` entry for CS.** See Recommendation note (a) above for the disposition of the existing (pre-narrative) edit-allowed tag.

## 2. Aconitase (ACO2) — `tca.aconitase`

**Gene:** ACO2 (UniProt Q99798, Aconitate hydratase mitochondrial, EC 4.2.1.3)
**Current skeleton PDB:** TBD_ACONITASE (bovine 1ACO candidate, 2.05Å)
**Already edit-allowed:** YES (APPROVED: TCA-RNG-CITRATE-PROCHIRALITY-01)

### Findings

1. **Known disease POINT mutation?** **YES — multiple confirmed.** UniProt Q99798 lists:
   - **S112R** (Ser112Arg) — ICRD; functional expression in yeast shows decreased function. dbSNP:rs786204827.
   - **G259D** (Gly259Asp) — ICRD. dbSNP:rs786204828.
   - **K736N** (Lys736Asn) — ICRD. dbSNP:rs786204829.
   - **L74V** (Leu74Val) — OPA9. dbSNP:rs141772938.
   - **G661R** (Gly661Arg) — OPA9. dbSNP:rs752034900.
   - ClinVar confirms: `29584` (S112R, **Pathogenic**, ICRD), `189312` (G259D, **Pathogenic**, ICRD).

2. **Mutant PDB?** **NO human ACO2 PDB exists** (verified: RCSB search for human + "aconitase" = 0 results). The skeleton uses **bovine 1ACO** (Bos taurus, 2.05Å, Lauble/Kennedy/Beinert/Stout 1994, J Mol Biol 237:437, PMID 8151704) as the teaching proxy. **Homology caveat:** bovine ACO2 and human ACO2 are highly homologous (both mammalian mitochondrial aconitases); the bovine structure is the canonical Ogston-hypothesis teaching structure. **The mutation S112R can be applied at runtime via `cmd.alter` on the bovine 1ACO structure** (sequence conservation at the active site is high), but the residue numbering should be cross-checked (bovine vs human numbering may differ slightly). The resulting structure has correct sequence but approximate geometry — `text_teaching` must note this.

3. **Reverse-mutation (the "correct fix"):** **S112R → R112S** (i.e. the player changes Arg112 back to Ser112). Alternative: G259D → D259G. S112R is the preferred candidate (functional characterization in yeast + ClinVar Pathogenic).

4. **Disease for narrative:** **Infantile cerebellar-retinal degeneration (ICRD, OMIM 614559)** — severe autosomal recessive neurodegenerative disorder; onset 2-6 months; truncal hypotonia, athetosis, seizures, optic atrophy, retinal degeneration; profound psychomotor retardation. Also **Optic atrophy 9 (OPA9, OMIM 616289)** — progressive visual loss. Strong narrative stakes ("the host infant is going blind and losing motor control").

5. **Source/claim_id CANDIDATE:**
   - `DIS-ACO2-01-cand` — ACO2 S112R causes ICRD. Source: UniProt Q99798 (variant S112R, PMID 25351951); ClinVar 29584 (Pathogenic); OMIM 614559. **CANDIDATE — pending human approval.**
   - `DIS-ACO2-02-cand` — ACO2 G259D causes ICRD. Source: UniProt Q99798 (variant G259D, PMID 25351951); ClinVar 189312 (Pathogenic); OMIM 614559. **CANDIDATE — pending human approval.**
   - `CAST-ACO2-PDB-01-cand` — Bovine 1ACO as teaching proxy for human ACO2 (with homology caveat). Source: RCSB PDB 1ACO (Bos taurus, 2.05Å, PMID 8151704). **CANDIDATE — pending human approval.**

### Recommendation
**Keep edit-allowed (already is). Add `edits.json` entry:** reverse-mutation S112R→R112S (preferred) or G259D→D259G. The existing edit-allowed status is **scientifically justified** (ICRD is a real ACO2 point-mutation disease). The bovine-1ACO homology caveat must appear in `text_teaching` and the human must approve the 1ACO teaching-proxy claim before content authoring.

## 3. Isocitrate dehydrogenase — THE IDH1/IDH2 vs IDH3A QUESTION — `tca.isocitrate_dh`

This is the dedicated section requested in the task brief. **The IDH3A question is RESOLVED by this research: IDH3A DOES have pathogenic missense mutations (RP90).** Both options are presented below for the human's science+narrative decision.

### 3A. Option A — IDH3A (the real TCA enzyme, NAD+-dependent mitochondrial)

**Gene:** IDH3A (UniProt P50213, Isocitrate DH [NAD] subunit alpha mitochondrial, EC 1.1.1.41)
**Current skeleton PDB:** 5GRF (Homo sapiens, αγ heterodimer, 2017, Ma/Peng/Huang/Ding, Sci Rep 7:40921, PMID 28098230)

1. **Known disease POINT mutation?** **YES — and this resolves the open question.** UniProt P50213 lists 8 RP90 variants, most marked "uncertain significance" — BUT ClinVar (deeper, more current) reveals **multiple Pathogenic missense**:
   - **M204I** (Met204Ile, c.612G>A) — ClinVar **Pathogenic**. dbSNP:rs2074705330.
   - **R316C** (Arg316Cys, c.946C>T) — ClinVar **Pathogenic**. dbSNP:rs770798851.
   - **A175V** (Ala175Val, c.524C>T) — ClinVar **Pathogenic**. dbSNP:rs765473830.
   - **M239T** (Met239Thr, c.716T>C) — ClinVar **Pathogenic**.
   - (Plus 2 nonsense Pathogenic: R361Ter, G155Ter — not usable as missense edits.)
   - The UniProt "uncertain significance" labels are stale relative to ClinVar; ClinVar is the authoritative clinical-classification source.

2. **Mutant PDB?** **NO mutant PDB** for any IDH3A RP90 variant. Wild-type 5GRF exists (in skeleton, verified Homo sapiens, Sci Rep 2017). Mutation must be applied at runtime via `cmd.alter` on 5GRF.

3. **Reverse-mutation (the "correct fix"):** **M204I → I204M** (preferred; ClinVar Pathogenic) **or** R316C → C316R.

4. **Disease for narrative:** **Retinitis pigmentosa 90 (RP90, OMIM 619007)** — autosomal recessive retinal dystrophy; night blindness + loss of midperipheral visual field; rod photoreceptor death followed by cone death. Narrative stakes: "the host is going blind."

5. **Source/claim_id CANDIDATE:**
   - `DIS-IDH3A-01-cand` — IDH3A M204I causes RP90. Source: UniProt P50213 (variant M204I); ClinVar (c.612G>A, Pathogenic); OMIM 619007. **CANDIDATE — pending human approval.**
   - `DIS-IDH3A-02-cand` — IDH3A R316C causes RP90. Source: UniProt P50213 (variant R316C, dbSNP:rs770798851); ClinVar (c.946C>T, Pathogenic); OMIM 619007. **CANDIDATE — pending human approval.**

### 3B. Option B — IDH1 / IDH2 (the cancer isoforms, NADP+-dependent)

**Genes:** IDH1 (UniProt O75874, cytosolic NADP+-dependent, EC 1.1.1.42); IDH2 (UniProt P48735, mitochondrial NADP+-dependent, EC 1.1.1.42)
**Note:** These are NOT the TCA-cycle enzyme (IDH3A is). They are NADP+-dependent, produce NADPH (not NADH), and are best known for cancer-associated neomorphic mutations.

1. **Known disease POINT mutation?** **YES — the famous cancer mutations.**
   - **IDH1 R132H** (Arg132His, c.395G>A, dbSNP:rs28931545) — abolishes Mg2+ binding, converts α-KG → R(-)-2-hydroxyglutarate (2-HG) instead of isocitrate→α-KG. Glioma (OMIM 137800). UniProt O75874 lists R132C/H/L/S/G as cancer variants. ClinVar `156444` = **Pathogenic/Likely pathogenic**.
   - **IDH2 R172K** (Arg172Lys, c.515G>A, dbSNP:rs121913503) — analog of IDH1 R132H; Yan et al NEJM 2009 (PMID 19228619). Glioma. ClinVar `375987` germline = "risk factor", oncogenicity = **Oncogenic**.
   - **IDH2 R140Q** (c.419G>A) — D-2-hydroxyglutaric aciduria 2 / AML.
   - These are the canonical IDH cancer mutants; extremely well-characterized in the literature.

2. **Mutant PDB?** **YES — IDH1 R132H mutant PDB 3INM exists** (verified: RCSB 3INM, "Crystal structure of human cytosolic NADP(+)-dependent isocitrate dehydrogenase **R132H mutant** in complex with NADPH, ALPHA-KETOGLUTARATE and CALCIUM(2+)", 2.1Å, 2009, Dang et al Nature 462:739, PMID 19935646, DOI 10.1038/nature08617). This is a real mutant structure (no `cmd.alter` needed). IDH2 has many wild-type PDBs (4JA8, 5I95, etc.) but none explicitly tagged R172K mutant.

3. **Reverse-mutation (the "correct fix"):** IDH1 **R132H → H132R**; IDH2 **R172K → K172R**.

4. **Disease for narrative:** Glioma / glioblastoma (brain cancer, OMIM 137800); D-2-hydroxyglutaric aciduria 2 (D2HGA2, OMIM 613657, IDH2). Very high narrative stakes ("the host has brain cancer").

5. **Source/claim_id CANDIDATE:**
   - `DIS-IDH1-01-cand` — IDH1 R132H causes glioma (neomorphic 2-HG production). Source: UniProt O75874 (variant R132H, PMID 19935646); ClinVar 156444 (Pathogenic/LP); PDB 3INM; OMIM 137800. **CANDIDATE — pending human approval.**
   - `DIS-IDH2-01-cand` — IDH2 R172K causes glioma. Source: UniProt P48735 (variant R172K, PMID 19228619); ClinVar 375987 (Oncogenic); OMIM 137800. **CANDIDATE — pending human approval.**

### 3C. The Science + Narrative Decision (FLAG FOR HUMAN REVIEW)

| Consideration | Option A (IDH3A — real TCA enzyme) | Option B (IDH1/IDH2 — cancer isoforms) |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific accuracy (is it the TCA enzyme?) | YES — IDH3A is the NAD+-dependent mitochondrial enzyme the TCA cycle uses | NO — IDH1 is cytosolic NADP+; IDH2 is mito NADP+. Neither is the TCA enzyme. |
| Skeleton PDB matches enzyme? | YES — 5GRF is IDH3A | NO — 5GRF is IDH3A; using IDH1 would require swapping to 3INM (and changing the node's enzyme identity) |
| Disease mutant confirmed? | YES — M204I, R316C (ClinVar Pathogenic) | YES — R132H, R172K (ClinVar Pathogenic/Oncogenic) |
| Mutant PDB structure exists? | NO (must `cmd.alter` on 5GRF) | YES — 3INM is the R132H mutant structure (no `cmd.alter` needed) |
| Narrative stakes | Medium — retinitis pigmentosa (blindness) | High — brain cancer (glioma) |
| Public recognition of disease | Low (rare recessive blindness) | Very high (IDH1 R132H is one of the most famous cancer mutations; FDA-approved targeted therapies exist) |
| Educational value (the game teaches real biochemistry) | Teaches the TCA enzyme itself (correct isoform) | Teaches the cancer-neomorph mechanism (2-HG oncometabolite) — a famous biochemistry story, but NOT the TCA reaction |

**Researcher's presentation (NOT a unilateral decision):** Both options are scientifically defensible. Option A is the **biochemically correct** choice (the node is "isocitrate DH in the TCA cycle" = IDH3A). Option B is the **narratively powerful** choice (the most famous cancer mutation in biochemistry, with a real mutant PDB), but it requires either (i) reframing the node as "the hero encounters a cancer-mutant IDH1 in a diseased cell" (scientifically the TCA-cycle IDH3A is not IDH1) or (ii) splitting the node into two (an IDH3A TCA node + an IDH1 cancer-side-encounter node). **The human must decide** based on the project's priority between biochemical-isoform-correctness and cancer-narrative-strength. See Open Questions.

## 6. Fumarase (FH) — `tca.fumarase`

**Gene:** FH (UniProt P07954, Fumarate hydratase mitochondrial, EC 4.2.1.2)
**Current skeleton PDB:** 5UPP (Homo sapiens, 1.8Å, 2018, verified)

### Findings

1. **Known disease POINT mutation?** **YES — many, well-characterized.** FH is one of the most-studied TCA disease genes. UniProt P07954 lists 15+ disease variants:
   - **R233H** (Arg233His, c.698G>A, dbSNP:rs121913121) — **HLRCC**; UniProt notes "catalytically inactive mutant; abolished ability to promote DNA repair." ClinVar `16236` = **Pathogenic** (Hereditary cancer-predisposing syndrome; Hereditary leiomyomatosis), **multiple submitters, criteria provided** (strongest evidence tier).
   - N107T, A117P, H180R, Q185R, K230R (FMRD+HLRCC), R233H, G282V, A308T (FMRD), F312C (FMRD), M328R, E362Q, D425V (FMRD), and more.

2. **Mutant PDB?** **NO mutant PDB.** Wild-type **5UPP** exists (in skeleton, verified Homo sapiens 2018). Mutation applied at runtime via `cmd.alter` on 5UPP.

3. **Reverse-mutation (the "correct fix"):** **R233H → H233R** (the strongest candidate — R233H is ClinVar Pathogenic with multi-submitter consensus AND UniProt explicitly notes "catalytically inactive," making it mechanistically the clearest "broken enzyme" for the narrative).

4. **Disease for narrative:** **Hereditary leiomyomatosis and renal cell cancer (HLRCC, OMIM 150800)** — predisposition to cutaneous/uterine leiomyomas + papillary type-2 renal cancer (~20% of patients). Also **Fumarase deficiency (FMRD, OMIM 606812)** — severe autosomal recessive encephalopathy with neonatal distress, metabolic acidosis, brain abnormalities. ## Open Questions for Human Review

1. **[CRITICAL] IDH3A vs IDH1/IDH2 (the IDH question).** Option A (IDH3A M204I, RP90) is biochemically correct and ClinVar-Pathogenic, but has no mutant PDB and a less-famous disease (retinitis pigmentosa). Option B (IDH1 R132H, glioma) is the most famous cancer mutation with a real mutant PDB (3INM) but is NOT the TCA-cycle enzyme (it is the cytosolic NADP+-dependent isoform). Decision needed: which does the game use? (Possibility: use IDH3A for the TCA node, AND add a separate IDH1 "cancer encounter" side-node — but that is a scope expansion requiring its own approval.)

2. **[CRITICAL] Citrate synthase disposition.** CS is currently edit-allowed (tag pre-dates restoration narrative) but has NO disease point mutant. Options: (a) keep the `edit:enzyme:tca.citrate_synthase` tag and reframe it as "structural exploration" (consistent with existing CAST-CSC-PDB-01 approval; the host's citrate synthase is NOT diseased — the hero just explores it), or (b) remove the tag. **Recommendation: (a)** — minimal disruption to the approved skeleton, and the restoration narrative simply does not apply at this node.

3. **[MEDIUM] α-KG DH (OGDH) deeper scan.** The negative finding (no pathogenic missense) is LOW-confidence — only the first 14 of 163 ClinVar Oxoglutaricaciduria records were examined (NCBI rate-limit forced a truncated scan). A complete scan of all 163 records (or a focused PubMed literature search for "OGDH pathogenic missense") may surface a confirmed pathogenic point mutation. If the human wants an OGDH edit-allowed node, commission a follow-up research pass. **Until then, do NOT make OGDH edit-allowed.**

4. **[MEDIUM] SUCLG1 vs SUCLA2 for the succinyl-CoA synthetase node.** Both subunits have confirmed pathogenic missense mutations (MTDPS9 / MTDPS5 respectively). The skeleton's PDB (6WCV) is SUCLG1, so SUCLG1 G85A → A85G is the no-PDB-swap choice. If the human prefers the SUCLA2 narrative (MTDPS5, encephalomyopathy, deafness/dystonia phenotype), the node's PDB would need to swap to 6G4Q. **Recommendation: SUCLG1 G85A** (matches existing skeleton PDB).

5. **[LOW] Aconitase residue numbering (bovine vs human).** The S112R mutation is in human ACO2 numbering. When applied via `cmd.alter` to the bovine 1ACO structure, the residue NUMBER may differ (bovine ACO2 is highly homologous but not identical). Phase 7 content authoring must map human S112 → bovine 1ACO residue number (e.g. via a sequence alignment) before writing the `edits.json` signature. The `target` field in the EditIntent uses `resi <N>`, so this mapping matters for the routing to work.
