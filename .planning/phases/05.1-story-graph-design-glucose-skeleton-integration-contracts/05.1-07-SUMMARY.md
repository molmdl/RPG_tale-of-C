## The 2OZL-to-6CFO Rationale

The current `pyr.pdh` cast candidate was `pdb:2OZL`. Per the research (05.1-RESEARCH-disease-mutants-glycolysis.md sections 2.3 + 4.3), the RCSB title for 2OZL is **"Human pyruvate dehydrogenase S264E variant"** — this is an **engineered phospho-mimic mutant** (S264E mimics regulatory phosphorylation, which inactivates the enzyme), used to study regulation. It is **NOT a wild-type structure** and is inappropriate as the wild-type cast for the restoration narrative (where the player should see the healthy enzyme, then apply the disease mutation).

The verified wild-type replacement is **`pdb:6CFO`** (2.70 A, human PDHA1 + acetyl phosphinate substrate analog, *Homo sapiens*, UniProt P08559) — from the same Whitley 2018 paper (PubMed:29970614) as the V138M disease-mutant structure **6CER** (CAST-PDH-MUTANT-PDB-01-cand, 2.69 A). 6CFO is a CANDIDATE pending Phase 7 approval.

The mutant-reveal approach (option b: load the real mutant PDB 6CER directly for accurate geometry, vs option a: apply-edit at runtime via `cmd.alter` for approximate geometry) is a **Phase 7 on_enter-content decision** — the skeleton loads ONE cast PDB per node in this replan; 6CER is NOT added to the skeleton here.

## The R479H (not R479W) Correction

The REPLAN-CONTEXT (section 2) example stated "PKLR R479W" as the pyruvate kinase disease mutation. The research **CORRECTED** this to **R479H** (dbSNP:rs118204085, UniProt P30613 VAR_011480):
- R479H is the verified Amish founder mutation (Kanno 1994, PubMed:8161798, "Molecular abnormality of erythrocyte pyruvate kinase deficiency in the Amish")
- R479H is structurally characterized (Valentini 2002, PubMed:11960989, "Structure and function of human erythrocyte pyruvate kinase. Molecular basis of nonspherocytic hemolytic anemia" — this paper specifically characterized CNSHA2 variants including R479H, G332S, G364D, D390N)
- R479W does NOT appear in UniProt's curated Swiss-Prot disease-variant list for P30613
- The reverse-mutation (the player's "correct fix") is **H479R**: `{"op": "point_mutation", "target": "resi 479", "args": {"new_res": "ARG"}}` (edits.json entry is Phase 7 content, NOT this replan)
- DIS-PKLR-01-cand is CANDIDATE pending Phase 7 approval

## anaer.ldh NOT Promoted (GSD11 is frameshift)

Per the research (section 2.4), GSD11 (LDH-M deficiency, MIM:612933) is a real disease, but NO confirmed pathogenic **missense** point mutation fits the `cmd.alter` edit mechanic:
- The canonical Maekawa-1990 mutation (PubMed:2334430) is a **20-bp frameshift deletion** (not a point mutation per the REPLAN-CONTEXT definition)
- The one ClinVar **Pathogenic** point mutation is **Q100Ter** (ClinVar 4715484) — a nonsense/truncation that is **mechanically incompatible** with `cmd.alter` (cannot un-truncate a 232-residue deletion; the Phase 4 EditRouter is built around `point_mutation` on existing residues)
- The REPLAN-CONTEXT example **R171H** (ClinVar 3599464) is a real variant linked to GSD11 but classified **Uncertain significance (VUS)** — using it would violate the no-fabricated-science rule

Therefore `anaer.ldh` stays a normal story node (the player still journeys through it — lactate fermentation is the anaerobic Good ending branch) but gains the `mc:observe` Observe second choice (Continue-to-MC) without any edit:enzyme tag, edit:offer choice, or DIS-*-cand claim. This matches the research recommendation + the plan.
