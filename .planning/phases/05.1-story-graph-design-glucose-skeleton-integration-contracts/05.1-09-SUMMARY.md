## The COX4I1-not-SURF1 Rationale

The research (05.1-RESEARCH-disease-mutants-etc.md §4) confirmed that for the Complex IV node (`etc.complex_iv`), the disease-mutant enzyme must be a subunit **present in the PDB structure 1OCC** (the candidate cast), because the restoration narrative's `on_enter` loads 1OCC and the player's edit targets a residue on a visible chain.

- **COX4I1** (cytochrome c oxidase subunit 4 isoform 1) IS present in 1OCC **chain D** (resi 130 = PRO wild-type) — it is the regulatory subunit. The disease mutation **T130P** (PDB mature numbering) / **P152T** (human UniProt precursor P13073 VAR_084182) causes **MC4DN16** (mitochondrial Complex IV deficiency, nuclear type 16) — a Leigh-spectrum phenotype (Pillai 2019, PMID 31290619; ClinVar 834063 Pathogenic). Hence **DIS-COX4I1-01-cand**.
- **SURF1** is an **assembly factor** (needed to assemble Complex IV but is NOT a structural subunit of the mature complex) and is **NOT present in the 1OCC structure**. A SURF1 edit could not target a residue on a 1OCC chain (the player would see nothing to edit). Hence COX4I1 was chosen, NOT SURF1, despite SURF1 being a well-known Leigh-spectrum gene.

The reverse-mutation (the player's "correct fix") is **P130T** (`{"op": "point_mutation", "target": "resi 130 and chain D", "args": {"new_res": "PRO"}}` — edits.json entry is Phase 7 content, NOT this replan).
