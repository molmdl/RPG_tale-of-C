## 3. The NADH-burden story — the science-vs-drama design problem (the segment's heart)

Ethanol is the only character whose pathway's central drama is not a carbon rearrangement but an **electron flood**: every ethanol the liver oxidizes hands the host TWO NADH whose re-oxidation depends on the ETC. This is the segment's distinctive dramatic engine, and it is fully source-grounded verbatim on S-ETOH (fetched live 2026-09-14):

**Drama-design mapping (what the player feels):**
- The **flood** = `alc.nadh_burden` (2 NADH; the TCA stalls). Dramatic register: the liver's wheel clogged by spent electron-carts; the hero's own soul-ferries (NADH) stacking up at the ETC's door.
- **The fork** = `alc.branch_fat_vs_burn`: bank the carbon as fat (→ `end.good.fatty_acid`) or push through ACSS2 into the shared wheel (→ `tca.entry` → True/Normal/Bad).
- **The backup** = at `alc.aldh2`, the NADH-stall's dark twin: the S-ETOH equilibrium-backup sentence lets teaching text honestly say the stalled cycle backs acetate-upstream pressure onto ALDH2 — and in an ALDH2\*2 host, acetaldehyde accumulates (the bad beat, §5.4).
- **Soul-jump alignment:** the True ending for alcohol is the flood RESOLVED — the two NADH unload their electrons into the ETC (approved claims), the protons pump, ATP synthase spins; meanwhile the carbon body rides acetyl-CoA through the same TCA as glucose's hero. No new ETC claims.

**Anti-confusion guardrails (mandatory, binding):** the two NADH carry the hero's ELECTRONS (soul), never the hero itself; the hero carbon travels ethanol → acetaldehyde → acetate → acetyl-CoA → citrate → …; the carbon NEVER becomes ATP, NADH, or "enters oxidative phosphorylation" (05.4-CONVENTION §3.6 template applies verbatim at every soul-transfer beat). Which of ethanol's two carbons the hero rides is deliberately unspecified (OQ-H2 — the methyl→acetyl-methyl / carbinol→acetyl-carbonyl mapping is standard biochemistry but NOT verbatim on fetched sources; keep hero-neutral or source it at approval, mirroring Phase 7's OQ-H discipline).
