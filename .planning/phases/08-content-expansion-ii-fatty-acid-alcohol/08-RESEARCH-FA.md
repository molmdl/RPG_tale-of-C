## 1. Summary

The FA character needs **~20 candidate claims** across **~10 new FA nodes** (8 pathway nodes + 2 new ending nodes, plus an `intro.shell_fa` edit in `intro.json`), reusing the entire shared TCA/ETC/ending arc verbatim (zero new TCA/ETC claims). The real regulated branch point — **CPT-1, inhibited by malonyl-CoA, the reciprocal switch between FA synthesis and FA oxidation** — maps exactly onto the canonical Good fork (store vs oxidize). Every claim in the segment is grounded on live-verified sources: the **LibreTexts Catabolism Beta-Oxidation page** (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, Darik Benson — same family as the approved Glycolysis/Fermentation pages), **Wikipedia "Beta oxidation"** (CC BY-SA, rev 1343709246, fetched live), and **J&F Ch 17.01/17.02 (Digestion/Mobilization/Transport; Oxidation of Fatty Acids)** — the SAME book family as the two already-approved sources (LIBRETEXTS-METAB-TCA = Ch 16; LIBRETEXTS-BIOCHEM-JF-CH19 = Ch 19), making Ch 17 a same-source-new-chapter approval (the §16.01-PDH precedent from Phase 7).

**Top findings that change the Phase 8 plan:**

1. **The FA edit-allowed floor (mechanics B-C3) is satisfiable with REAL structures.** ACADM (medium-chain acyl-CoA dehydrogenase, the canonical FA-oxidation disease) carries: **1EGE = WILD-TYPE human MCAD, 2.75 Å** (verified WT by coordinate scan: NO SEQADV records; its siblings 1EGC/1EGD carry the T255E/E376G crystallization mutations), and **4P13 = the K304E disease-mutant structure, 1.73 Å** (SEQADV: PDB GLU 304 ← UniProt LYS 333). The K304E allele (ClinVar **VCV000003586, Pathogenic/Likely pathogenic**, "criteria provided, multiple submitters, no conflicts"; c.985A>G, the prevalent "G985" mutation — PMID 7904584, DOI 10.1159/000154157) is THE most common MCAD-deficiency allele. MCAD deficiency is "the most common fatty acid β-oxidation disorder" (Wikipedia Beta oxidation, Clinical-significance section, verbatim, fetched live) — the segment's signature disease story with a real restorable mutation on a real human WT cast.
2. **OCTN2 (SLC22A5) HAS a structure — post-training-data.** My prior knowledge said "no OCTN2 structure"; live RCSB verification found **9PDQ (2.72 Å cryo-EM, human OCTN2 bound to carnitine, occluded conformation; Davies et al., Nat Commun 17:181, 2025, DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-66867-6, PMID 41318751)** plus 9PFB/9PMD (3.06/2.99 Å). This gives the carnitine-supply node a human cast and grounds the CDSP (systemic carnitine deficiency) disease story with **P46S (ClinVar VCV000193250, Pathogenic/Likely pathogenic, multiple submitters, no conflicts)**.
3. **The J&F Ch 17.02 page carries both the segment's best verbatim stoichiometry AND two sentences that must NOT be quoted:** the body text states "a 16:0 fatty acid (for example) is converted to a (14):0 fatty acid and the 2C molecule acetyl-CoA… undergoes six more rounds of the β-oxidation cycle until the entire 16:0 fatty acid is fully converted to **8 acetyl-CoAs**" (the exact palmitate stoichiometry needed), but ALSO contains "ultimately yielding approximately **106 ATP**" (banned numeric yield) and a sloppy "In each repetitive cycle of this pathway, acetyl-CoA and **one CO2** are released" (scientifically wrong for β-oxidation — CO2 release happens in the TCA, not the spiral). Source-quality flags are in §8; the claim texts below quote only the verified-correct sentences.
4. **The storage side (FA's Good ending) has real 2020s human structures:** DGAT1 — **6VYI (3.0 Å cryo-EM, human DGAT1 with oleoyl-CoA ligand 6OU; Sui et al., Nature 581:323–328, 2020, DOI 10.1038/s41586-020-2289-6, PMID 32433611)** — and GPAT1 — **8E4Y (3.4 Å cryo-EM, human GPAT1; Johnson et al., Nat Struct Mol Biol 30:22–30, 2023, DOI 10.1038/s41594-022-00884-7, PMID 36522428)**. DGAT1's disease story (DIAR7) has only an uncertain-significance point variant → DGAT1/GPAT1 are exploration casts (no disease arc), like citrate synthase.
5. **CPT-2 has NO human structure; CPT-1A has NO full structure; CACT and ACSL1 have NO structures at all** (UniProt xrefs fetched live: P23786 empty; P50416 = 2LE3 only, an NMR structure of the N-terminal regulatory segment, NOT the enzyme; O43772 empty; P33121 empty). The shuttle nodes are narrative-only (the `tca.akg_dh` precedent) or cast with the RAT CPT-2 structure **2DEB (1.6 Å, X-ray; DOI 10.1016/j.str.2006.01.008, PMID 16615901)** as a labeled family stand-in (the bovine-aconitase precedent). A false-memory warning: PDB **2H8P is a potassium channel, NOT CPT-2** (verified live — never rely on memory for IDs).
6. **The anaerobic rule (framing (d)) needs one new FA-specific claim + one new Bad node.** β-oxidation produces NADH whose only mammalian re-oxidation route is the ETC; without O2 the NAD+ pool collapses and the spiral stalls → energy-crisis Bad. This honors PROJECT.md row 100: "FA + alcohol reach only the Bad-ending trigger (O2-depletion energy crisis)".
